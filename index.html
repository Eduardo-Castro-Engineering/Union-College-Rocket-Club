About us

The Union College Rocket Club (UCRC) brings together students across majors to explore rocketry, from 3D modeling and payload design to hands-on building, fabrication, and launching. No experience is needed. We hold regular hobby launches, and our competition team builds a rocket from scratch each year for the national Battle of the Rockets competition.

We also run activities that introduce local kids to science and engineering through rocketry.

April 2026: our scratch-built rocket Charger took first place in the Deployable Payload Event at Battle of the Rockets in Culpeper, Virginia. Read the story.
